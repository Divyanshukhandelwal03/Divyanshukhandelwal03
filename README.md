### 🚀 Featured Projects & Products Developed

#### 1. 🎯 [OneFightMore](https://www.onefightmore.in/) — India's Smartest Govt Exam Preparation Suite
> *Live Platform: [https://www.onefightmore.in](https://www.onefightmore.in/)*  

An end-to-end edtech and AI study ecosystem built for competitive aspirants (SSC, Banking, UPSC, Railways) supporting 70+ government exams.
- **AI Scorecard OCR & Weak-Topic Diagnosis**: Multimodal OCR engine that parses candidate scorecards from Testbook & Oliveboard, classifying silly mistakes vs. conceptual gaps.
- **Targeted CBT Error Retest Engine**: Generates targeted retests from past mistakes for 100% concept closure and benchmarks qualification probability via the proprietary **OFM Index™**.
- **🧩 OneFightMore Chrome Snipper Extension**: Custom browser extension engineered specifically for OneFightMore to let students capture, crop, and auto-sync online mock test questions and solutions directly into their error notebook with 1 click.
- **📱 OneFightMore Mobile Snipper APK**: Dedicated Android application enabling students to snap offline questions and scorecard photos on the go, running OCR text extraction and syncing them to their profile.
- **Integrated Study Productivity Suite**:
  - Deep Focus Pomodoro Timer with ambient soundscapes (Lo-Fi beats, soft rain, study cafe).
  - GitHub-style daily habit consistency heatmaps & drag-and-drop weekly timetable planner.
  - Scientifically-backed **SM-2 Spaced Repetition Flashcards** for active recall revision.
  - SSC Typing Speed Test Arena with real-time WPM, accuracy, and error tracking.
- **Architecture**:
  - `onefightmore-frontend`: Modern, responsive Next.js / React application with PWA offline support, dark mode, and Chrome Extension manifest.
  - `onefightmore-backend`: Scalable Node.js / Express and Python OCR backend with secure APIs, database indexing, and AI analytics pipelines.
